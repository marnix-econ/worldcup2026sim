Projected Group Finishes

How each group is projected to end

Each group's four teams are ranked 1st through 4th by their average finishing points across all trials (using average goal difference, then Elo, as tiebreakers — mirroring FIFA's group hierarchy). The share in the last column is the probability that the team actually finishes in that projected position; a low number means the placement is a coin-flip, not a lock.

Projected Knockout Bracket

If the projected group tables hold

This takes the projected group finishes above, seeds them into FIFA's real bracket via the Annex C third-place table, then plays every tie out by always advancing the more likely winner. Each match box shows that team's chance of advancing — including via a penalty shootout — so you can see which ties are near coin-flips (shown in amber). The bracket reads inward from both sides toward the Final in the centre.
